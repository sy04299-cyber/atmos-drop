The idea

Every room holds water you cannot see. Atmos Drop cleans the air, catches that water and gives it back to you, ready for whatever needs it.

We live indoors

People spend about 90% of their time indoors, where some pollutants run 2 to 5 times higher than outside (U.S. EPA, U.S. data).

Water is wasted

Air conditioners and dehumidifiers pour the moisture they collect down the drain. In humid rooms, offices and factories, that water could be put to use.

Filters are guessed at

Most reminders count days. A clogged filter changes airflow, and Atmos Drop senses that.

Where it works

Anywhere the air holds water

Atmos Drop cleans the air and collects the moisture it removes, so the water is available wherever you need it. The plant in our pictures is only one example.

Desks, homes and offices

Cleaner air at your workspace, with a small, steady supply of water for plants, cleaning or topping up a container.

Pre-order open

Commercial buildings

Offices, shops and hotels with air conditioning pass a lot of humid air. The water can serve non-drinking needs such as cleaning, flushing and garden irrigation, while the air gets cleaner.

Pilots in development

Factories and workshops

Production areas with moist air hold more water to collect. Each site needs its own assessment, because industrial air can carry contaminants beyond what a standard filter handles.

Pilots in development

Gardens, greenhouses and nurseries

Collected water can feed irrigation directly, so plants are watered from the humidity around them.

How does the pre-order work?

You reserve a unit for ₹999. When it is ready to ship, we tell you the delivery timing and the balance due. You pay the balance only then, and you can cancel for a full refund of your reservation any time before that.

When will it ship?

We have not set a date yet, because we are still testing the prototype. We will update you at each step and give notice before any balance is due.

What can I use the water for?

Any non-drinking use: watering plants, cleaning and washing, toilet flushing, or topping up equipment where the water quality suits it. You choose where it goes.

Can I drink the water it collects?

No. The condensate is not purified or tested for drinking.

Can it be used in a factory or a commercial building?

That is the direction we are building toward. The pre-order unit today is a desk-scale model. We are developing larger versions and are open to pilots with factories and buildings. Industrial air can contain contaminants a standard filter is not designed for, so we assess each site first. Tell us about yours below.

How much water does it collect?

That depends on your room's temperature and humidity: very humid spaces hold more water to collect than dry ones. Small Peltier collectors yield modest amounts: one published study measured a maximum of 24 mL per hour in optimal conditions. We have not yet published our own measurements and will after testing.

What does it filter?

Particles such as dust and pollen, and odours and many VOCs through activated carbon. It is not designed to remove carbon monoxide, so please keep a CO alarm.

What about warranty and support?

Warranty and support terms will be published before units ship, and you will see them before you pay the balance.
